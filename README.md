## Before you submit

1. **Make a small shortlist.** Play a few games from each destination and compare genre, controls, session length and device support.
2. **Prepare the right build.** Browser portals need a working web version. Stores and console programs require their own supported builds. Have a playable link, screenshots, controls and a concise description ready.
3. **For publishers, add the business details.** Include your deck, gameplay video, development stage, timeline, budget and the help you want.
4. **Check the agreement before distributing widely.** Review exclusivity, territory, release windows, source-code delivery, SDK/ads rules, fees, payout thresholds and regional eligibility. A non-exclusive deal at one destination does not override an exclusive deal elsewhere.

Selection, payment and traffic are never guaranteed. A platform’s total audience is not the audience promised to an individual game. Public developer pages can remain live while intake changes; recheck the route when you are ready to submit.
