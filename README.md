**10 places to explore for your next browser game.**

Find publishers, game portals and distribution networks in one place—with direct developer links and a quick explanation of how each opportunity works. Start by playing a few games on a destination, then check whether your game fits its audience and publishing requirements.

## Before you pitch

- **Prepare a browser build.** A mobile app package alone is not a web game. Check the destination’s supported exports, controls and loading requirements.
- **Make it easy to try.** Have a working playable link, a short description, screenshots and clear controls ready.
- **Choose the right deal.** A license fee pays for agreed rights; revenue share depends on earnings; a marketplace or community page offers a place to publish.
- **Check release restrictions.** Review exclusivity, SDK requirements, allowed links and ads, payout conditions, and whether an existing release affects eligibility before submitting to several places.

Selection, payment and traffic are never guaranteed. A platform’s total audience is not the audience promised to an individual game.
